# **App Name**: Plant Paradise

## Core Features:

- Plant Journal: Maintain detailed logs, notes, and photo entries to track each plant's progress over time.
- AI Plant Identification: Identify unknown plants from a photo, providing their name, description, and care information using AI. The AI will act as a tool, referencing a database of plant information.
- GreenAI Health Diagnosis: Analyze plant photos for potential health issues like over or underwatering using AI. The AI will act as a tool by consulting a database of diseases and symptoms.
- AI Chat Assistant: A built-in chatbot that answers plant care questions, using journal information to give personalized advice. The AI will act as a tool that takes care history into account.
- Marketplace: Buy and sell plants, seeds, and gardening tools with other users.
- Achievements: Earn badges and certificates for plant care milestones.
- Quirky Reminders: Send fun reminders to water plants (in English and Hinglish). Examples: Aaj mujhe paani nahi mila toh kal tu guilt se roye ga, Hydration check! Tere plant ko bhi chai nahi, paani chahiye. Oye hoye! Your plant is looking drier than my jokes! Water me!

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Forest green (#388E3C) to reflect plant life and nature.
- Background color: White (#FFFFFF) to keep the overall feel clean, light, and calm.
- Accent color: Earthy brown (#A1887F) to highlight secondary actions.
- Headline font: 'Belleza' sans-serif. Body font: 'Alegreya' serif.
- Use custom-designed icons that reflect growth, nature, and care.
- Clean, card-based design to present plant information clearly.
- Gentle animations for plant growth tracking and reminders.
